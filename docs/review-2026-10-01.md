# Read-through review of 2026-10-01: what can go, what can be simpler, what is good

Four reviewers (kernel; net, abi and applib; cpu, boards and hopos; drivers,
media, apps and tools) read the whole tree against the handbook and against
the Go tree in `OLD/`. Nothing was changed. Derek's expectation was right:
what is 1-to-1 Go (partmem, pool, conport, handoff, watchdog, the lifecycle,
nat, the switcher asm, stage2, mve, vitals) is good, and there is little to
gain there. The lines are in three things: helpers rewritten per crate,
board facts living as a cfg forest in hopos instead of on the Board trait,
and code that was written but never proven or called (Go leftovers,
diagnostics from finished hunts, EDID, AML).

Total achievable, counted conservatively: about 4,000 lines of production
code removed, or written once instead of three times, without losing
behavior; most of it low risk. The list below is the work queue; ALLES.md
keeps only what must be done.

## 3. Weekend (structural, medium risk)

- **The contract in the trait.** hopos calls fifteen methods that are not
  on the Board trait but are looked up via `vboard::` with cfg, and the
  same thing has a different name per board (`boot_param` versus `config`,
  five signatures for temperature, `wdt` versus `watchdog`). In the trait:
  150 to 190 lines of cfg out of watchdog, telemetry, bench, flip, cage and
  net, and the Deref forwarding of O6N and Altra gone (`Uefi<M: Machine>`
  like `Raspi<S: Soc>`).
- **One interrupt model on ARM.** gicv3 does not implement
  `cpu::irq::Controller` (gicv2, aic and plic do): three boards write their
  own claim and EOI loop and uefi has its own line table with a transmute.
  200 to 250 lines; the irq path on O6N and Radxa, so on hardware.
- **memattr as the only remap**, with break-before-make (the Arm ARM
  requires it on a change of memory type) and the PXN rule: Apple's second
  remap and the rk3566 wrapper gone, and `tail_rings` one function. 100
  lines. On hardware on two boards.
- **The ring promise in one place.** Now five places for "the kernel maps
  this Normal" (`RINGS`, `HOST_RINGS`, two `tail_rings` modules,
  cage_riscv). `HOST_RINGS` Hardware everywhere (also the Radxa, where
  kernel RAM is Normal and the promise is still Maintained: the 930 MB/s
  case of the M4), `RINGS` gone, each board a `map_tail_normal`. Measure
  with the RX run on the Radxa.
- **One arch-free rotation and resident list** (`Roster`): now three times
  the rotation and four times the list (el2, riscv, cage_riscv, the test
  model). 150 lines and one semantics. QEMU arm64 and riscv must run on it.
- **The `Reader`/`Writer` traits in `net::ring`** have one real impl plus a
  fake ring; Go tested over real rings and applib already does. 250 to 300
  lines and the generics out of the switch; the Device branch of the Radxa
  then gets tests for free.
- **abi as the single source of truth.** The wire format of the system API
  is in abi, kern/system and applib/sys; the net plan in abi, net/plan and
  applib/contract; `kern::Slot` and `kern::Region` next to those of abi;
  system.rs with its own `read_header`, `Call` and `encode_resp`. Together
  300 to 400 lines.
- **What exists three times:** SHA-256 (kernel twice, cpu, cloudflared:
  move to `abi::checksum`, 250), `FlowState` and `NatState` (kernflip and
  nat, 50), `kern/src/stage2.rs` next to `cpu/src/el2/stage2.rs` (240 plus
  300 test), the fixed-buffer `fmt::Write` in eight places
  (`bounded::Text<N>`, 120), the wait loop with `delay` in fifteen drivers
  (`dev::spin_until`, 80), the acceptor and worker pool in three apps
  (140), the virtio handshake in net and blk (80), dwmac and dwmac4 as one
  crate like stmmac (150 to 200), the AML helpers three times (60), the
  DSDT reader in uefi/facts next to fw (120).
- **The flip recorder:** steps nobody writes, an agent field that is always
  empty but encoded, bounded, tested and logged (40); the UEFI facts page
  as a `#[repr(C)] struct` instead of 25 atomics that the reader counts by
  hand (80). `flip::reset()` does an SMC itself that probably does nothing
  on the M4 (no EL3): reset belongs to the board; check on the M4 first.
- **The lifetime of a slot** lives in four places (generation, mounts,
  job, base and size, quarantine): one `Life` per slot (50).
- **tools/lib.sh**: `port()` of 18 lines in 14 scripts, the cleanup trap,
  objcopy, `probe()` in three boot ports. 600 to 700 lines. And merge the
  riscv Hop script into qemu-test-hop.sh with `ARCH=` (250).
- **One line to measure:** the executor touches 512 slots every round with
  a swap (read-modify-write, executor/src/lib.rs `step`); load first, then
  swap. The OS core is in every measurement in full, so this can count.

## 4. Choices for Derek

- `regex.rs` in cloudflared-lean: 750 lines of Pike VM for path rules that
  no jobspec uses. Refuse with a reason, or keep.
- AML in fw (300 lines) versus Go's `nic_intid` table: two sources for an
  INTx fallback that runs on no board (O6N does MSI-X, Altra polls on
  purpose). Keep the Go shape (430 removed) or keep AML (30 removed).
- The IRQ path of tg3 (150 lines): wire it up like Go did on 2026-09-19, or
  remove it while the M4 polls.
- rtl8126 variants that sit on no board (8125D, CP, BP, DASH, 75 lines):
  removing them gives a loud `UnknownChip` on another O6N revision.
- `sync::Stop` and `WakerSet`: the stop doorbell never rings, but pump and
  switch register on it every round (a CAS). Touches handbook §2.
- `qemu-soak-hop.sh` (497 lines): the hunting tool of 2026-09-30; the
  regression is now guarded by `qemu-test-slowdisk.sh`.
- The `DeviceGrant` abstraction with one user (fbgrant): Go shape on
  purpose.
- Map the pool memory on Apple and the Radxa as Normal instead of Device:
  then `map_tail_normal`, `tail_rings` and the exception in `RINGS`
  disappear entirely. High risk: the SError cause on the rk3566 and the
  cause on Apple must first be proven gone.
